# TSS Ecosystem Roadmap & Brand Guidelines

## 1. Journey Pipeline
`Student` ➔ `Verification` ➔ `Community` ➔ `Learning` ➔ `Projects` ➔ `Events` ➔ `Startup` ➔ `Funding` ➔ `Hiring` ➔ `Founder` ➔ `Mentor` ➔ `Investor` ➔ `Gives Back`

## 2. Onboarding & Verification Flow
- **Entry**: Website Homepage.
- **Role Selection**: Choose from Student, Founder, Recruiter, HR, Mentor, Investor, Freelancer, Creator, Campus Ambassador, Volunteer, Startup, Company.
- **Registration**: Dynamic registration form based on chosen role.
- **Verification**: Email/phone verification + manual admin review.
- **ID Generation**: On admin approval, automatically generate a unique TSS Member ID, QR Code, and Digital Member ID Card.
- **Access Unlock**: Official access links (WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn, Instagram).
- **Resume Upload**: Drive link/URL configuration.
- **Hiring**: Resume profile forwarded to partners for job eligibility.

## 3. Resume Studio Specification
- Provide three clean template types: FAANG, Startup, General.
- Enforce one-click PDF downloading.

## 4. TSS Verification Levels
- **Verified Student**: Manually vetted.
- **Verified Resume**: Checked public URL link.
- **Verified Portfolio**: Checked social/experience portfolios.
- **Verified Skills**: Verified skill credentials.
- **Verified Project**: Project verification during Build challenges.
- **Verified Startup**: Incubated startup validation.
- **Verified Recruiter**: Recruiter identity check.
- **Verified Company**: Partner organization review.
- **Verified Mentor**: Corporate mentor vetting.

## 5. Build Challenge Sandbox
- **Monthly Cycle**: Community submits problems ➔ Team selects Top 3 ➔ Kickoff Google Meet ➔ 30 days building sandbox ➔ Weekly status reviews ➔ Demo Day ➔ Winners announced ➔ Startup support, mentorship, and resources provided.
- **Signature Event**: After 10 Build challenges, conduct the **TSS Real Problem Hackathon**.

## 6. Monthly Metrics Tracker
- Track: New verified members, Active members, Job applications, Interviews scheduled, Offers received, Students hired, Startups launched, Problems solved, Projects completed, Companies partnered, Colleges partnered, Sponsors acquired, Revenue, Event attendance, Mentor sessions, Community retention.
